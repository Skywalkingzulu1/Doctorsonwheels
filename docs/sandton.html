Sandton FAQs

Booking and pricing questions only — this page does not give medical advice.

How do I book an online doctor consultation in Sandton?

Create an account, choose video or chat, pick an available doctor, and consult online. Home visits can be requested where a participating practitioner is available in your suburb.

How much does an online consultation cost?

Video consultations start from R300, follow-up visits start from R500, and doctor home visits start from R800. Final pricing is confirmed before you pay.

Are the doctors HPCSA registered?

Yes. Doctors on the platform are HPCSA-registered and verified before they can consult.

Do you offer home visits in Sandton?

Home visits are offered where a participating practitioner is available. Availability varies by suburb and time of day, so request early for same-day visits.

Can I get a prescription after an online consultation?

Where clinically appropriate, the consulting doctor can issue a digital prescription to your preferred pharmacy, along with referrals or sick notes.

What should I do in a medical emergency?

Do not use online consultations for emergencies. Call South African emergency services on 10177 or go to your nearest emergency department.
