Account Deletion Request

Request the deletion of your personal data from the Doctors on Wheels platform

Submit Your Request

Fill in the details below and we'll process your account deletion within 14 business days, as required by POPIA.

What Happens When You Request Deletion

  1. Account Deactivated — Your account is immediately deactivated upon receiving your request.
  2. Data Reviewed — Our team reviews your request and identifies all personal data associated with your account.
  3. Data Deleted — Personal data is permanently deleted, subject to legal retention periods (see below).
  4. Confirmation — You'll receive a confirmation email once the process is complete.

Data Retention (POPIA & HPCSA)

We comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) guidelines. Certain data may be retained for legal and regulatory purposes:

  • Medical Records: Retained for a minimum of 6 years from the last consultation date, as required by HPCSA.
  • Financial Records: Retained for 5 years as required by South African tax law.
  • Communication Logs: Anonymised usage logs may be retained for platform security and analytics.

After the applicable retention period, all remaining data is permanently and irreversibly deleted.
